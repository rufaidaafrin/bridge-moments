# AI Showcase Submission

## Project title

Bridge Moments

## Preview description

Bridge Moments is an AI-powered "What Happens Next?" and speech/AAC bridge for people with Down syndrome, autism, speech delays, or intellectual disabilities. A caregiver can describe an upcoming stressful moment, such as a dentist visit, school change, haircut, loud public place, or sudden routine change, and the app turns it into a complete support kit: a simple visual story, sensory forecast, first-then plan, plan-change rescue script, speech phrase builder, intent decoder for unclear sounds/pointing/objects/hand-leading, communication board, EasyWorld text translator, and shareable "My Voice Passport" support card.

I built it because my younger sister has Down syndrome and is still developing her speech. Sometimes she may say sounds instead of full words, point at something, bring an object, or grab someone's hand to show what she wants, but it can still be hard to understand. Bridge Moments is designed to make confusing moments easier to understand, treat every sound or gesture as communication, and give caregivers practical ways to figure out what someone may need before frustration builds.
