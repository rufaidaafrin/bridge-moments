# AI Showcase Submission

## Project title

Bridge Moments

## Preview description

Bridge Moments is an AI-powered independence, transition, and speech/AAC bridge for people with Down syndrome, autism, speech delays, or intellectual disabilities. It includes My Mode, a simple screen with large text-and-visual cards, optional real photos, and one-tap speech so someone can communicate needs like help, water, bathroom, pain, quiet, or calling family. Caregivers can add custom cards that save in the browser.

A caregiver can also describe stressful moments like a dentist visit, school change, haircut, loud public place, or sudden routine change. The app turns it into visual stories, sensory forecasts, first-then plans, plan-change rescue scripts, speech/AAC phrase support, an intent decoder for unclear sounds, pointing, objects, and hand-leading, an EasyWorld translator, and a shareable My Voice Passport.

I built it because my younger sister has Down syndrome and is still developing her speech. I want Bridge Moments to help people communicate and understand more on their own, while still helping caregivers support them respectfully.
